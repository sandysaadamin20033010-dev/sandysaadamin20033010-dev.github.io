About me

A personality on camera, a strategist behind it

I'm Sandy Saad, a content creator and presenter based in Alexandria, Egypt, currently a Content Creator & Marketing Intern at GoWondaa, a UK-based AI no-code website builder, where I plan, script and film content for its expansion into the Egyptian market.

I also make Business Stories, my own Arabic video series about how famous companies started, and I speak at events, so being in front of people, on camera or on stage, is where I'm most at home.

What drives me: people. I love work where I can feel the impact, connect with an audience, and see a brand come alive through a story.

My business side is real too: at Modonty I developed 15 collaboration opportunities and reached 21 Techne Summit 2026 speakers, and I coordinate business development and marketing at iGCC and the Orange Corners Egypt startup incubation program.

My path started early: coordinating marketing inside a startup incubator, facilitating entrepreneurship workshops, and even keeping the books in accounting offices, which taught me how businesses actually run behind the scenes.

That mix is my edge: a creator who understands business goals, PR and partnerships, not just the edit. I find the opportunity, start the conversation, then tell the story.
